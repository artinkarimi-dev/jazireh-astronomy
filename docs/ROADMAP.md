# Product Roadmap

This roadmap is public-safe and intentionally distinguishes shipped work from planned product direction.

## Current Foundation

The current repository already provides the foundation of the product:

- responsive Persian RTL public frontend
- WordPress-backed content management
- scientific news
- latest selected Jazireh videos
- NASA APOD
- Sky page
- Explore page with authored educational object content
- Radar simulation
- Jazireh Daily archive with external Community synchronization
- newsletter storage

## Near-Term Product Scope

Likely next steps consistent with the current architecture:

- richer Sky Tonight data
- broader live or near-real-time scientific panels
- stronger editorial category and topic experiences
- more operational staging and production automation
- improved search and discovery
- production scheduling for Jazireh Daily sync

## Future Expansion

Longer-term ideas that fit the product direction but are not represented as complete in the current code:

- Sun Now and live solar imagery
- Earth-from-space experiences such as NASA EPIC-derived surfaces
- earthquake and event feeds
- topic hubs and deeper scientific browsing
- saved content or personalization
- notifications and recurring user-return loops
- English-language product expansion
- stronger app-like or PWA capabilities

## Guiding Principle

Jazireh is being developed as a repeat-visit scientific portal rather than a one-off content website. The roadmap favors features that make the experience feel current, explorable, and worth returning to over time.
